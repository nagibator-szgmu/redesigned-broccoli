# MEDSIM V2.3 ARCHITECTURE & CLINICAL REASONING SPECIFICATION

## 1. Overview & Vision
MEDSIM V2.3 elevates the clinical simulation platform into a full **Clinical Reasoning Simulator**. The simulator shifts from isolated tools to a structured, cycle-based clinical reasoning pipeline grounded in real physiological responses and diagnostic evidence.

```
PATIENT STATE
      ↓
ABCDE PRIMARY ASSESSMENT
      ↓
DERIVED PROBLEM LIST (Syndromes & Vital Instabilities)
      ↓
CLINICAL HYPOTHESES & DIFFERENTIAL REASONING
      ↓
EVIDENCE MODEL (Observed Evidence vs. Missing Tests)
      ↓
DIAGNOSTIC & INTERVENTION PLAN
      ↓
PHYSIOLOGICAL SIMULATION TICK
      ↓
REASSESSMENT & TRAJECTORY TRACKING
      ↓
UPDATED DIFFERENTIAL & FINAL DIAGNOSIS
      ↓
SAFETY ANALYTICS & COMPREHENSIVE DEBRIEF
```

---

## 2. Core Architectural Principles & Invariants

1. **Deterministic Physiological Source of Truth:**
   - No fake or hardcoded ad-hoc physiological mutations.
   - All vital changes flow through the existing simulation engine (`patient.js`, `deterioration.js`).
   - Reassessment strictly observes and compares actual `ps` snapshots over time.

2. **Strict Terminology & Educational Heuristics:**
   - Differential scoring is an **educational heuristic ranking** (индекс соответствия), never represented as a "clinical probability" or "Bayesian posterior probability".
   - The UI explicitly renders the educational disclaimer: *«Учебный эвристический рейтинг, не клиническая вероятность.»*

3. **Separation of Problem List and Etiological Diagnosis:**
   - A **Problem** is an objective physiological syndrome (e.g. *Hypoxemia: SpO2 86%, Tachypnea: RR 31/min*).
   - A **Diagnosis** is the underlying disease pathology (e.g. *Острый инфаркт миокарда, ТЭЛА*).
   - Problem lists are derived from objective patient data without pre-judging the etiology.

4. **Evidence Model Integrity:**
   - Distinguishes clearly between *Observed Evidence* (completed diagnostic tests with abnormal/normal findings) and *Missing Evidence* (indicated tests not yet performed).
   - An unperformed test is **never** presented as a negative test result.

5. **Non-Invasive Safety Analytics:**
   - Safety classification (`critical_error`, `major_error`, `suboptimal`, `missed_opportunity`, `appropriate`) serves as educational debrief feedback and does not alter the baseline scoring formula (`computeScore`).

## 3. Data Entities & Flow

### 3.1. Reassessment Engine (`src/engine/reassessmentEngine.js`)
Pure function: `evaluateReassessment(baselinePS, currentPS)`
- Compares: `hr`, `sbp`, `dbp`, `map`, `rr`, `spo2`, `temp`, `gcs`, `pain`.
- Computes: `delta`, `direction` (`improved` | `worsened` | `unchanged` | `not_assessable`), `statusSummary`.
- Guard: $MAP$ is evaluated only when $SBP > 0$ and $DBP > 0$.

### 3.2. Problem List Engine (`src/engine/problemListEngine.js`)
Pure function: `deriveProblemList(ps, cd, revealedResults)`
- Derives active clinical problems:
  - `respiratory_failure` (Hypoxemia / Tachypnea / Bradypnea)
  - `hemodynamic_instability` (Hypotension / Shock / Severe Hypertension)
  - `cardiac_dysfunction` (Tachycardia / Bradycardia)
  - `neurological_deficit` (Altered Mental Status / Coma / Depressed GCS)
  - `severe_pain` (NRS Pain Score $\ge 5$)
  - `thermal_dysregulation` (Hypothermia / Severe Hyperthermia)
  - `laboratory_syndromes` (e.g. Myocardial Injury, Acidemia, Coagulopathy from revealed tests)

### 3.3. Safety Analytics Engine (`src/engine/safetyEngine.js`)
Pure function: `evaluateClinicalSafety(cd, selTreat, selDiag, appliedFx, revealedAnamnesis)`
- Classifies interventions into:
  - `critical_error`: Administration of dangerous contraindications (`wrongTreat`).
  - `major_error`: Administration of contraindicated drug without checking life history.
  - `missed_opportunity`: Omission of life-saving interventions (`needTreat`) or vital tests (`needDiag`).
  - `suboptimal`: Ordering redundant or invasive tests without primary stabilization.
  - `appropriate`: Timely administration of indicated treatments.

### 3.4. Clinical Trajectory & Checkpoints
Checkpoints recorded on timeline:
- `INITIAL`: On patient admission / case start.
- `REASSESSMENT`: On user-initiated reassessment click.
- `POST_INTERVENTION`: Following key medication or procedural stabilization.
- `FINAL`: Prior to case submission and routing.
